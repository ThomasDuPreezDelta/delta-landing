How We Build Your Brand

A collaborative, stage-by-stage process designed to uncover who you are, define how you look, and equip your team with everything they need to show up consistently.

Step 1

Brand Canvas Workshop

We begin by getting under the skin of your venture — where you are today, who you're speaking to, and what makes you different. The Brand Canvas becomes the strategic foundation everything else is built on.

Step 2

Moodboard Session

Before a single pixel is designed, we align on visual direction. We explore references, styles, and aesthetic territories together, so when design begins it's built on shared vision, not assumption.

Step 3

Look & Feel

With direction agreed, we bring your brand to life visually. Colour, typography, imagery style, and design language — refined until it feels unmistakably yours.

Step 4

Brand Identity

We present your complete brand identity — logo suite, visual system, and the rationale behind every decision. Built to impress investors, attract talent, and scale with your venture.

F.A.Q.

All your questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about our design process, timelines, and how we build legacy brands for modern founders.

contact our team
How long does a typical brand identity project take?
Most brand identity engagements run between 4–8 weeks depending on scope. A focused identity sprint (logo, palette, type, guidelines) typically completes in 4 weeks. Full brand strategy + identity + web typically runs 8–12 weeks.
What's included in a Brand Identity Guidelines document?
The guidelines cover logo usage and clear space rules, full colour system (primary, secondary, and neutrals with HEX/RGB/CMYK values), typography system, photography and illustration direction, tone of voice principles, and brand application examples across digital and print.
Do you handle trademark registration for logos?
Absolutely. We offer brand evolution engagements where we retain and refine what's working while building a more complete and consistent system around it. We'll assess your existing assets in the discovery phase and recommend the right approach.
Can we update our existing logo instead of a full rebrand?
Brand strategy defines the positioning, purpose, and personality of your brand — the "why" and "who". Brand identity is the visual and verbal expression of that strategy — logo, colour, type, tone. We recommend doing both together; identity without strategy produces a brand that looks good but says nothing.
What is the difference between brand strategy and identity?
We work across all stages. For pre-seed companies we offer a focused Brand Sprint format that delivers a core identity system quickly and affordably. As you grow, that foundation scales with you.
