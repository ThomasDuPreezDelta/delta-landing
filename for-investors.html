About Apex

Curated deal flow from inside the ecosystem

Apex is an invite-only investor community connecting experienced angels, VCs, and family offices with Europe's most promising ventures. Members gain exclusive access to curated deal flow, strategic co-investment opportunities, and direct engagement with founders.

Beyond capital, investors contribute expertise, mentorship, and strategic guidance to shape the next generation of companies — while building meaningful relationships within the ecosystem.

Why join Apex

Built for serious investors

Six advantages of being inside Europe's most active early-stage startup campus.

Curated deal flow

Receive exclusive introductions to ventures aligned with your thesis. Every deal is vetted and personalised based on your investment preferences, stage focus, and sector expertise.

Early access

Be the first to meet founders from our Studio and Services programmes. Get early visibility into high-potential ventures before they hit the broader market.

Community & events

Join investor roundtables, founder showcases, and exclusive networking events. Build relationships with fellow investors and the entrepreneurs they back.

Collaborative network

Co-invest alongside experienced angels and serial entrepreneurs. Share due diligence, leverage collective expertise, and access better deal terms through syndication.

Priority access to experts

Your portfolio companies gain direct access to our network of specialists across product, growth, sales, engineering, and operations — at the right time.

Visibility & partnerships

Get involved as a mentor, advisor, or speaker at our events. Build your brand within the ecosystem while supporting the next generation of founders.
