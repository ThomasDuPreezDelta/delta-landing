Membership types

Two ways to get involved

Supporting founders through on-campus engagement or across the wider ecosystem.

Ecosystem mentor

Ecosystem Mentor

A broad group of experienced founders, operators, and advisors who support ventures remotely or in-person when there's a strong fit.

  • Curated introductions based on your expertise
  • Invited to lead panels, masterclasses, and events
  • 2 day passes per month to the campus
  • Remote or in-person engagement

Limited spots available each cohort.

Resident mentor

Resident Mentor

A select group of 10 mentors per quarter who are actively embedded in the on-campus community and directly engaged with founders.

  • Everything from Ecosystem Mentor
  • Host 1:1 Office Hours at Delta Campus Berlin
  • Complimentary Delta Campus Pro Membership
  • Direct access to on-site founders and ventures
  • Actively engaged in community and on-site events

10 resident mentors selected per quarter.

FAQ

Common questions

Time commitment varies by tier. Ecosystem Mentors typically dedicate 2–4 hours per month, while Resident Mentors are more actively involved with on-campus office hours and events. We work around your schedule and only make introductions that are genuinely relevant to your expertise.

Ecosystem Mentors engage with founders remotely or selectively in-person, based on matched introductions. Resident Mentors are a select group of 10 per quarter who host regular office hours on-campus and receive a complimentary Pro Membership. Residents have direct, ongoing access to our founders and ventures.

No. Ecosystem Mentors can be based anywhere — we facilitate remote introductions and virtual sessions. Resident Mentors do need to be able to attend The Delta Campus Berlin regularly, so Berlin proximity is preferred for that tier.

We look for depth over breadth: operators who've built and scaled companies in a specific domain — product, engineering, sales, fundraising, growth, or operations. We prioritise people with founder experience or senior operator roles at high-growth companies. The best mentors are those who've made the mistakes they're helping founders avoid.

Ecosystem Mentoring is uncompensated — it's about giving back and staying connected. Resident Mentors receive a complimentary Delta Campus Pro Membership (valued at over €500/month). In some cases, mentors may negotiate equity or advisory arrangements directly with founders — we facilitate but don't govern these.
