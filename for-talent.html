Why The Delta

Work where the best companies are built

The Delta isn't a job board. It's a curated network of ambitious startups and ventures — many still in the early stages where your work will genuinely shape the company's trajectory.

Whether you're a designer, engineer, growth operator, or generalist looking for a high-impact role, we connect exceptional talent directly with the founders who need them most.

Why join

Work that matters, from day one

What makes working inside The Delta ecosystem different from a standard job search.

Early-stage impact

Join a company where your contribution shapes the direction of the product and business — not just a ticket in a backlog. At early-stage ventures, every role is foundational.

Curated introductions

We don't send your CV to 50 companies. We make targeted introductions between exceptional people and the specific ventures where they'll thrive.

Access the ecosystem

Being part of The Delta network means access to events, mentors, investors, and a community of 800+ founders and operators — regardless of which company you join.

Grow fast

Startups move faster than corporates. If you want to level up your skills, take on more responsibility, and see the direct results of your work, this is the right environment.

Vetted companies

Every company in The Delta ecosystem has been through a selection process. You're not searching blindly — you're choosing from a curated set of serious ventures.

Find your co-founder

Many operators who join The Delta network go on to found their own ventures. If building is your ambition, this is where you find the founders and ideas worth betting on.

How it works

Four steps to your next role

01

Join the network

Register your profile with The Delta talent network. Tell us about your background, skills, and the type of role and company you're looking for.

02

Get matched

We review your profile and identify the ventures in our ecosystem that are the strongest fit — based on stage, sector, role type, and culture.

03

Meet the founders

We make a warm introduction to the relevant founders and hiring leads. No cold applications, no ghosting — you meet people who already want to talk to you.

04

Find your place

If there's a mutual fit, you join a high-growth team inside one of Berlin's most curated startup ecosystems — with access to the full Delta community from day one.
